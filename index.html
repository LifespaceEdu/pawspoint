Six skills to get your dog travel-ready

Station

Go to a mat or bed and settle — the foundation skill

Look

Eye contact on cue — focus amidst chaos

Tuck

Curl up in a small space — airplane and car ready

Chin

Chin rest for calm — settle without a mat

Middle

Position between your legs — crowded spaces

Feet

Paws on handler's feet — stay close in lines
