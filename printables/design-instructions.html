What to do

1
Answer the questions at the bottom of this page

Think about the skill before you draw anything. What does the dog actually need to learn?

2
Use the blank screens to draw your lesson

You have 3 screens. Use as many as you need. Draw where the text goes, where pictures go, and what each part of the page says. Label everything.

3
Write the training steps on the lines below the screens

These are the actual instructions someone would follow to teach their dog. Be specific enough that a person who has never trained a dog could do it.

4
Trade with your partner and check each other's work

Could you teach a dog this skill using only what they drew and wrote? If something is confusing, tell them what's unclear.

Drawing tips

  • Boxes and labels are fine. This is not an art project.
  • Use arrows to show what someone taps or where they scroll
  • Stick figures work great for showing the dog and handler
  • If you want an image somewhere, just draw a box and write what the image shows
  • Write the actual words you want on the screen, not just "text goes here"

Think first, then draw

  1. What is the skill called, and what does it look like when the dog does it right?
  2. Why would a traveler need this skill? When would they actually use it?
  3. Break the training into steps. How does a beginner start? (Think: what did WE do first?)
  4. How does the owner know the dog has learned it? What does success look like?
