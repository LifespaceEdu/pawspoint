Station

Teach your dog to go to a mat or bed and settle — no matter where you are.

Why this matters for travel

Your dog needs a "home base" anywhere — an airport lounge, a hotel room, a friend's house. Station teaches your dog that the mat IS the calm spot, even when everything else is new and exciting.

Training Steps

Step 1: Lure to the mat

Place the mat on the floor. Use a treat to lure your dog onto it. The moment all four paws are on the mat, mark and reward.

Step 2: Add the cue

Once your dog goes to the mat reliably with the lure, say "Station" right before you lure. After 10-15 reps, try saying "Station" without the lure.

Step 3: Build duration

Ask for Station, then wait 5 seconds before rewarding. Gradually increase to 30 seconds, then 1 minute, then 5 minutes.

Step 4: Add distance

Send your dog to Station from 3 feet away, then 6 feet, then across the room. The dog should go to the mat on cue from a distance.

Step 5: Proof in new environments

Take the mat to a new room. Then the backyard. Then a quiet park. Then a busy store. Each new place, start back at shorter duration.

How do you know it's working?

Your dog goes to the mat on one cue, lies down, and stays for 5+ minutes while you move around — in a place they've never been before.
