Look

Teach your dog eye contact on cue — focus amidst chaos.

Why this matters for travel

In airports, busy streets, and new places, your dog needs to check in with you instead of reacting to everything around them. "Look" gives you a way to recapture your dog's focus instantly.

How do you know it's working?

Your dog makes eye contact within 2 seconds of the cue, even with distractions nearby — and holds it for at least 5 seconds.
