Middle

Teach your dog to position between your legs — crowded spaces.

Why this matters for travel

Crowded terminals, farmers markets, busy sidewalks — Middle puts your dog safely between your legs where they're protected from foot traffic and can focus on you instead of the chaos.

How do you know it's working?

Your dog moves between your legs on one cue and stays there while you stand still or walk slowly — even with people passing close by.
