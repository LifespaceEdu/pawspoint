Chin

Teach your dog a chin rest for calm — settle without a mat.

Why this matters for travel

Sometimes there's no mat. Chin rest teaches your dog to settle by placing their chin on your hand, your knee, or a surface — an instant calm signal that works anywhere.

How do you know it's working?

Your dog rests their chin on your open hand on one cue and holds it there calmly for 30+ seconds, even in a new environment.
