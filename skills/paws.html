Paws

Teach your dog paws on handler's feet — stay close in lines.

Why this matters for travel

Standing in line at check-in, waiting at a gate, queuing for food — Paws keeps your dog glued to your feet so they don't wander, sniff strangers, or get stepped on.

How do you know it's working?

Your dog places their front paws on your feet on one cue and stays there while the line moves — without needing to be re-cued each time you step forward.
